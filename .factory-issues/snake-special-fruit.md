# Add a rare special fruit

Add a special kind of fruit that occasionally appears during play.

- It must be rare rather than continuously available.
- It must remain on the board for only a short amount of time and disappear if it is
  not collected.
- It must be visually obvious and clearly distinguishable from the normal fruit.
- Collecting it must grow the snake by 10 lengths.
- Normal fruit and existing gameplay must continue to work.
